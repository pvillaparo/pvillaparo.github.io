The working paper presented was "Voice Messages and Early Immunization: A Field Experiment?" We conduct a field experiment in the metropolitan area of the Dominican Republic to study the impact of sending voice messages on early immunization behavior. We found significant spillover effects from the Vice-President voice messages.
